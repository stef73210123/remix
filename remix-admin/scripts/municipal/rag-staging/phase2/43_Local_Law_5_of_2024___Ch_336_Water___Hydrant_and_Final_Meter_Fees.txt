

TOWN OF NORTH CASTLE 
 
Local Law No. __5__ For the Year 2024 
Adopted September 11, 2024 
 
 
A Local Law to amend Chapter 336, Water, of the Code of the Town of North Castle to permit the 
collection of hydrant fees in Water District #1, 2, 4, 5 and 8 on customer water bills, as set forth in 
Master  Fee  Schedule via quarterly  billings  to  customers.    In  addition,  a  local  law  to  permit  the  
collection of a final meter reading fee in all water districts. 
 
BE IT ENACTED by the Town Board of the Town of North Castle as follows: 
 
Section 1.  Create the following new Section in the Town Code as follows: 
 
§ 336-55.1 Final Water Meter Reading Fee. 
 
Upon request for final meter readings or upon the transfer of title to any property which is subject 
to water service, the owner shall pay a final meter reading fee in such amount as set forth in the 
Master Fee Schedule. 
 
Section 2.  Create the following new Section in the Town Code as follows: 
 
§ 336-109.1 Final Water Meter Reading Fee.

to water service, the owner shall pay a final meter reading fee in such amount as set forth in the 
Master Fee Schedule. 
 
Section 3.  Create the following new Section in the Town Code as follows:

to water service, the owner shall pay a final meter reading fee in such amount as set forth in the

Section 4.  Create the following new Section in the Town Code as follows:

Upon request for final meter readings or upon the transfer of title to any property which is subject

Section 6.  Create the following new Article VIII in Chapter 336 of the Town Code as 
follows: 
 
Article VIII Water District No. 8 
 
RESERVED 
 
 
Section 7.         Create the following new Article IX in Chapter 336 of the Town Code as 
follows: 
 
Article IX Hydrants 
 
Section 336-400.  In Water District #1, 2, 4, 5 and 8 the collection of hydrant fees shall be charged 
as set forth in the Master Fee Schedule via quarterly billings to customers. 
 
Section 8.  Conflicting Standards. 
 
Where the requirements of this Local Law impose a different restriction or requirement than imposed 
by other sections of the Code of the Town of North Castle, the Town Law of the State of New York or 
other applicable rules or regulations, the requirements of this Local Law shall prevail.  
 
Section 9.  Severability. 
 
The invalidity of any word, section, clause, paragraph, sentence, part or provision of this local law 
shall not affect the validity of any other part of this local law that can be given effect without such 
invalid part or parts. 
 
Section 10.  Effective Date. 
 
This Local Law shall take effect immediately upon its adoption and filing with the Secretary of State.
