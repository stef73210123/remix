

TOWN OF NORTH CASTLE 
 
Local Law No. __6__ For the Year 2025 
Adopted December 10, 2025 
 
A Local Law to override the tax levy limit established in General Municipal Law 3-  c. 
Be It Enacted by the Town Board of the Town of North Castle as fo llows: 
Section 1. Legislative Intent 
 
It is the intent of this local law to override the limit on the amount of real property taxes that may 
be levied by the Town of North Castle, County of Westchester pursuant to General Municipal Law 
Section 3-  c, and to allow the Town of North Castle, County of Westchester to adopt a town budget 
for  (a)  town  purposes;  and  (b)  any  other  special  or  improvement  district  governed  by  the  Town  
Board for the fiscal year 2026 that requires a real property tax levy in excess of the "tax levy limit" 
as defined by General Municipal Law Section 3-  c. 
Section 2. Authority 
 
This local law is adopted pursuant to subdivision 5 of General Municipal Law Section 3-c, which 
expressly authorizes the Town Board to override the tax levy limit by the adoption of a local law 
approved by vote of sixty (60%) percent of the Town Board. 
Section 3. Necessity 
 
The following items reflect a 4% increase and do not include salary and other usual increases: 
 
1. Loss of approximately $500,000 in interest inco me. 
 
2. Increase in  pension contributions of approximately 8%. 
 
 
Section 4. Effective Date 
 
This local law shall be effective immediately upon filing of same with the Secretary of State of 
the State of New York.
