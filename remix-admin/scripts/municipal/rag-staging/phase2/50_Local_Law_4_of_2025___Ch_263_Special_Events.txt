TOWN OF NORTH CASTLE 
 
Local Law No. __4__ For the Year 2025 
Adopted October 22, 2025 
 
A LOCAL  LAW  to  update  Chapter  263  “Special  Events”  to  remove  the  public  hearing  
requirement. 
 
 BE IT ENACTED by the Town Board of the Town of North Castle as follows: 
 
Section 1.  Amend Section 263-3.E(1) of the Town of North Castle Town Code as follows: 
 
 (1)  A sketch showing the area to be occupied by the proposed amusement rides. 
 
 
Section 2.  Amend Section 263-4.L of the Town of North Castle Town Code as follows: 
 
Notice. 
 
Prior to the start of the event, notice of the event shall be sent by first class mail to all property owners 
within a distance of 250 feet from all sides of the property. 
 
Section 3.  Amend Section 263-10 of the Town of North Castle Town Code as follows: 
 
The Town Board may modify or waive any of the provisions or requirements within this chapter if the Town 
Board determines that the goals of this chapter are better served thereby. 
 
Section 4.  Conflicting Standards. 
 
Where the requirements of this Local Law impose a different restriction or requirement than imposed by other 
sections of the Code of the Town of North Castle, the Town Law of the State of New York or other applicable 
rules or regulations, the requirements of this Local Law shall prevail.  
 
Section 5.  Severability. 
 
The  invalidity  of  any  word,  section,  clause,  paragraph,  sentence,  part  or  provision  of  this  local  law  shall  not  
affect the validity of any other part of this local law that can be given effect without such invalid part or parts. 
 
Section 6.  Effective Date. 
 
This Local Law shall take effect immediately upon its adoption and filing with the Secretary of State.
